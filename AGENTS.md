# OSINT project guardrails

- Keep collection limited to public sources whose terms and access rules have
  been reviewed. Never bypass authentication, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, rate limits,
  or technical access controls.
- This project is for source-backed public-interest and defensive research, not
  private-person dossiers, doxxing, stalking, or covert surveillance.
- Do not add email/phone/home-address/person entity types without a separate
  privacy and legal review.
- Preserve provenance for every observation: source, URL, collection time,
  publication time when known, content hash, and confidence.
- Do not store full scraped pages by default. Prefer citations, hashes, and
  short reviewed summaries.
- Active checks are out of scope unless the user explicitly scopes assets they
  own or are authorized to assess.
- Do not push or deploy the new project without explicit user approval.
